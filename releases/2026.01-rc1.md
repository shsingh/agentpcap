## The gate rule

**M001 passing with M003's full matrix, at M002's cost, is the result
that decides the project's public claims.** Three receipts close it:

- **M001 passes**: recall 1.0 on the known and the shadow classes at
  [PR #110](https://github.com/shsingh/agentpcap/pull/110), merged
  `8fa8f71`.
- **M003 reports its full matrix**: FOUR transports run real served
  exchanges (`eval/agentpcap_eval/m003.py`), with coverage tied to each
  run's own records; the legacy-SSE row lands covered on the per-flavor
  park face (the fix rides [PR #114](https://github.com/shsingh/agentpcap/pull/114)).
- **At M002's cost**: the identical harness rides the release binary;
  the wall delta −0.23% and the tap CPU 2.164% sit under the published
  3% figure at [PR #111](https://github.com/shsingh/agentpcap/pull/111),
  merged `dbe8c70`.
